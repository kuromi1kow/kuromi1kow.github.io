**Status:** under review at [*Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/engineering-applications-of-artificial-intelligence) (Elsevier).

An answer can be supported by the retrieved evidence while the citations attached to it point somewhere else. This study evaluates the two separately, using matched evidence in a local scientific RAG setting.

It is the companion to my first-author study on [literal-match reranker supervision](/publication/2026-literal-match-reranker-supervision). Details will be posted after review.
