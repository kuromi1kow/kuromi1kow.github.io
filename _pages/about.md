I'm an MS student in [Artificial Intelligence at Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/cs/masters/program/ai/), funded by the [**Bolashak Presidential Scholarship**](https://www.bolashak.gov.kz/en/o-stipendii). I study **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**: whether the answers of large language models are actually supported by the evidence they cite, and how to train systems so that they are. I'm grateful to receive research mentorship from [Prof. Aaron Mueller](https://aaronmueller.github.io/) at Boston University.

**I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.**

Before graduate school I was the primary ML engineer at the [National Bank of Kazakhstan](https://nationalbank.kz/en), where I built a retrieval and AI-agent system over 80,000+ financial and legal documents. I also teach AI and data science as a part-time lecturer in [Astana Hub](https://astanahub.com/)'s Tech Orda program.

**Interests:** large language models, RAG, agentic AI, reasoning, efficient AI systems.
